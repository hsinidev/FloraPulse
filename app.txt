App Name: FloraPulse
Version: 1.0.0
Platform: Android / iOS
Category: Indoor Plants / AI Diagnosis
Target Market: Global
Price Model: Free 5 diagnoses/month + $4.99/month Pro

Description:
A specialized indoor plant care journal that uses your phone camera to analyze leaves for color changes, diagnosing watering, lighting, humidity, or nutrient issues instantly.

Key Features:
- Plant collection with photos, species, pot date
- AI camera diagnosis: point at leaf → get instant diagnosis
- Diagnoses: overwatered, underwatered, too much sun, root bound, nutrient deficiency
- Smart watering schedule based on plant type + your humidity zone
- Fertilization reminders with seasonal adjustments
- Growth photo timeline (weekly photo log showing plant growth)
- Species database: 500+ common houseplants with care guides
- Community: share plant photos and get tips from other plant parents

Target Audience: Plant enthusiasts aged 22-40, apartment dwellers, plant collectors
Competitors: PictureThis, Planta, Greg
Unique Value: Real-time camera diagnosis vs schedule-only apps
